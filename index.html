The League

Not a pickup game.
A league.

RUFL takes Ultimate Football on Roblox and gives it everything a real league has: teams with front offices, a full season, standings that matter and a championship with money on it.

$200 on the line

FAQ

Questions?

What game does RUFL play?

Ultimate Football on Roblox. All league games are played there.

Do I need a team to join?

No. Join the Discord as a free agent. Teams are always looking for players, and the free-agent channel is where they look.

What is the Ultimate Bowl?

The championship game at the end of every RUFL season. This season the winning team plays for $200.

Can I join in the middle of a season?

Yes. Teams sign players during the season, so join the Discord and get on the free-agent board.

Where do I find the schedule and results?

In the Discord. Schedules, scores and announcements are all posted there.
